01

Foster School of Business: marketing operating model and AI workflow transformation

Overview

In a high-volume, matrixed marketing environment, execution broke down more from how the organization operated than from what the brand said. Stakeholder requests arrived with uneven context and unclear priorities, creating avoidable rework and slowing execution.

My role

As Director of Marketing, I identified intake quality, prioritization, and workflow clarity as high-leverage opportunities. I led the work across marketing operations, AI-enabled workflow design, governance, stakeholder alignment, and team adoption.

The work

Mapped the existing intake and execution workflow to find where ambiguity and missing context slowed progress. Built an AI-assisted intake system using OpenAI, Zapier, and Asana that prompted stakeholders for clear objectives, audiences, deadlines, and success criteria before work entered the pipeline.

Created clearer prioritization and reporting structures connecting marketing activity to business goals, budget decisions, and leadership visibility.

A more disciplined operating model across a nine-person team and a $2M budget: clearer inputs, fewer avoidable handoffs, stronger alignment.
