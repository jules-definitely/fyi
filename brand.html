01

Foster School of Business rebrand

Overview

Foster faced a fragmented user experience and an identity that undersold its value in a competitive graduate education market. Partner-led content decisions had created inconsistencies across web, email, and social.

My role

As Director of Marketing, I led strategy, alignment, and execution alongside a cross-functional team of designers, content creators, and project managers. I partnered with leadership on narrative and with our Director of Design on visual identity.

The work

  • Market and messaging strategy: validated product-market fit and refined personas around high-intent decision drivers.
  • Positioning and brand system: new architecture and guidelines aligning internal teams and partner touchpoints.
  • Agency oversight: selected and managed external agencies against business strategy.
  • Content and funnel strategy: an end-to-end content ecosystem built into the website structure.
  • Brand activation: new branded merchandise as a tangible expression of the refresh.

"Best Rebrand," UCDA Annual Design Awards

A more differentiated, premium identity in graduate business education, with a messaging system that gives the team a foundation for continued lifecycle work.

02

Donuts Inc. → Identity Digital rebrand

Overview

Donuts Inc. operated under a name that no longer reflected the scope or ambition of the business. As the company expanded its category leadership, the brand needed an identity that could carry a broader, more credible story to partners, media, and customers.

My role

As Senior Marketing Manager, I was part of the core marketing team through the rebrand, focused on partner engagement, content strategy, and market positioning alongside an external creative agency.

The work

  • Maintained partner and community trust through the transition.
  • Collaborated with the creative agency on market research and positioning.
  • Represented marketing's perspective in the renaming process.
  • Led social and PR strategy introducing the new identity to media.

The repositioning held partner trust through the transition and set up the reseller conversions that followed, turning 6+ pilot partnerships into recurring revenue relationships.
